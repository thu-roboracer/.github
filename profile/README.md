# THU RoboRacer

We are a student club at **Tsinghua University** building and programming a 1/10-scale autonomous race car for the [RoboRacer](https://roboracer.ai/) competition (formerly F1TENTH).

Our car drives itself: it sees the track with LiDAR, works out where it is, plans a racing line and overtakes opponents, all running on an onboard computer with ROS 2.

## What we work on

- **Hardware:** chassis, electronics, power and safety systems
- **Software:** perception, localization, planning and control in ROS 2
- **Simulation:** testing our software on virtual tracks before it runs on the real car
- **Operations:** sponsors, events and recruiting

## Join us

We welcome Tsinghua students from every department, whether you write code, build things, or want to help organize. No racing or robotics experience needed.
